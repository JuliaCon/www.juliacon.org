# Julia Community Prize

The Julia Community Prize 2026 winners have been announced! Winners receive a certificate of accomplishment and a cash award of USD 1000.

## 2026 Community Prize Winners: 

### Penelope Yong
For her work on Turing.jl.

### Andreas Noack, Daniel Karrasch, and Jishnu Bhattacharya
For their work on LinearAlgebra.jl.

## Awards Committee

The members of the committee for 2026 are Prof. Alan Edelman, Prof. Tim Holy, and Mr. Curtis Vogt.
